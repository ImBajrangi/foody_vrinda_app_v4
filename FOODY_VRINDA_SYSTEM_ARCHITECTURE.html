Foody Vrinda: How The Entire System Works

From placing an order to cooking, delivery, cash settlement & ratings.

🛒
1. The Customer (Order & Tracking)
Simple, fast, and no repetitive typing
  • Must Login First: Nobody can place fake guest orders. One tap via Google or Phone.
  • Saved Once, Pre-filled Forever: Phone number and delivery address are saved on the first order. Next time, it's 1-tap checkout.
  • Live Map Tracking: Customer sees real-time preparation status and delivery rider moving on the live map.
  • Delivery OTP: Customer gets a 4-digit secret code on screen. Rider only gets "Order Completed" when customer shares this code.
🍳
2. The Kitchen (Cooking & Hot Food Magic)
Why food NEVER gets delivered cold
  • Only Their Own Orders: Each kitchen screen only sees dishes for their branch.
  • Hot Food Innovation (Parallel Dispatch): The moment chef clicks "Start Cooking", the system alerts the nearest rider right away. While the chef is frying/packing, the rider is driving to the shop. When food is ready, rider is already at the gate!
  • Pickup OTP: Kitchen screen shows a 4-digit code. Rider must tell this code to pick up the parcel (0% wrong parcel delivery).
  • Auto-Close: If kitchen staff goes offline or store closes, orders are automatically blocked so customers don't wait.
⚡ 15-Min Urgency Alert: If kitchen ignores an order for 15 minutes, Owner gets an emergency alarm. At 30 minutes, COD orders auto-cancel so customer isn't kept waiting.
🛵
3. Delivery Sarathi (The Rider)
Nearest radar assignment + 2-step security
  • Nearest Rider Alert: The closest active rider gets the order ping first.
  • Step 1 (Pickup): Gives Pickup code to Kitchen staff to collect parcel.
  • Step 2 (Delivery): Takes Delivery code from Customer at doorstep to finish order.
💰
4. Cash on Delivery (Evening Settlement)
Clear daily cash accounting without disputes
  • Daily Digital Wallet: Rider collects cash from customers all day. System tracks exact total.
  • Evening Handover to Shopkeeper:
    • Paid in Full (100%): Account cleared to ₹0. Rider gets +15 Trust Points.
    • Paid Partial (Shortage): Remaining balance stays as debt. 24h grace period, late penalty applies, and -20 Trust Points deducted.
    • Paid Extra: Extra money is saved in Rider's advance wallet.
📊
5. CIBIL Trust Score (300 to 900 Points)
Good workers get rewarded, bad habits get stopped
Who? How Score Goes UP? 📈 How Score Goes DOWN? 📉 If Score Drops Too Low
🛵 Rider Fast delivery, timely evening cash deposit Short cash deposit, cancelling accepted orders High-value COD orders blocked
🍳 Chef Accepts in <5 mins, 5⭐ hot food reviews Late cooking, 15-min alerts, food complaints Performance bonus cut, Owner audit
👤 Customer Smooth order acceptance, online payments Refusing COD order at doorstep, fake orders COD permanently banned (Prepaid only)
⭐
6. Separate Ratings for Chef & Rider
Fair credit where it's due
  • Chef Rating (1–5 ⭐): Rate the taste, freshness & packing. (e.g. Piping Hot, Pure Desi Ghee)
  • Rider Rating (1–5 ⭐): Rate the delivery speed & behavior. (e.g. Super Fast, Polite & Humble)
  • Direct Impact: 5-star ratings give bonus Trust points directly to that Chef and Rider!
