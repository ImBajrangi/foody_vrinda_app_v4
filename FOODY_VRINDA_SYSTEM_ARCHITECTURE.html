Foody Vrinda Ecosystem

Enterprise Multi-Role Business & Technical Guide

Complete Operational Blueprint for Customer, Kitchen, Sarathi & Owner

1. Customer / Devotee Workflow
Ordering & Seamless Auth Flow Mandatory Auth Gate
  • No Guest Orders: Customer must login/signup before placing an order. Guest shoppers are prompted with instant 1-tap Google/Phone login upon clicking checkout.
  • One-Time Phone & Address Save: During first login, customer provides mobile number and street address with GPS pin. This data is permanently saved in Supabase Postgres and auto-prefilled on all future orders.
  • Real-time Live Order Tracking: After order placement (COD or Online), customer tracks order live via interactive Map HUD & dynamic floating capsule.
  • Delivery Verification OTP: A unique 4-digit code is displayed on customer's tracking screen. Handing over this OTP to the rider completes delivery.
  • 24/7 Dedicated Support: 1-Tap WhatsApp & Phone support available directly on the tracking screen.
2. Kitchen Staff / Chef Workflow
Distributed KDS & Smart Hot-Food Dispatch Parallel Early Dispatch
  • Distributed Scoped Access: Staff only sees orders belonging to their assigned shop_id.
  • Hot Food Guarantee (Parallel Early Dispatch): When kitchen taps "Start Preparing", the system immediately alerts the nearest online Delivery Sarathi. By the time the meal is cooked (10-15 mins), the rider is already at the kitchen door! Zero waiting, food delivered piping hot.
  • Kitchen Auto Online/Offline: Kitchen is online only if within Shop Operating Hours (e.g. 08:00 AM – 10:30 PM) AND at least one staff is On Duty. If staff goes offline or shop closes, new orders are blocked automatically.
  • SLA Escalations:
    • 15-Min Unaccepted Alert: Notifies Owner & Developer with emergency sound and message.
    • 30-Min Auto-Cancel: COD orders unaccepted for 30 mins are cancelled automatically with apology reason sent to customer.
  • Rider Pickup OTP: Kitchen screen displays a 4-digit Pickup OTP. Rider must verify this OTP to collect the order.
3. Delivery Sarathi (Fleet Rider) Workflow
Nearest Dispatch & 2-Way OTP Security Dual OTP Protected
1
Nearest Rider Broadcast
GPS radar finds nearest on-duty riders and sends sound alert when kitchen starts cooking.
2
Pickup OTP at Kitchen
Rider provides Pickup OTP to Kitchen staff to verify parcel before departure.
3
Delivery OTP at Doorstep
Rider asks customer for 4-digit Delivery OTP to mark order successfully delivered.
4. Daily COD Cash Settlement Engine
End-of-Day Flexible Settlement & Penalties Audit Ledger
  • Daily Digital Cash Ledger: Rider collects cash throughout the day; total is tracked in their digital "Cash-in-Hand" balance.
  • End-of-Day Reconciliation with Shopkeeper:
    • Full Payment: 100% cash deposited ➔ Ledger cleared to ₹0, Rider awarded +15 CIBIL Trust Points.
    • Partial Payment (Shortage): Remaining balance recorded as Unsettled Debt. 24h grace period applies, penalty/interest accrues daily, Rider deducted -20 CIBIL Points.
    • Excess Payment: Credited to Rider's advance wallet balance.
5. Universal Role CIBIL / Trust Score System

Every role (Customer, Rider, Chef, Shop Owner) maintains a dynamic Trust Score (Scale: 300 – 900 Points) that governs priority, limits, and penalties.

Role + Score Boost Factors - Score Penalty Factors Consequence of Low Score
🛵 Delivery Sarathi Full daily cash settlement, 5⭐ reviews, fast delivery Unsettled cash debt, cancelled orders, late delivery Blocked from high-value COD orders, dispatch freeze
🍳 Kitchen Chef <5 min acceptance, 5⭐ taste/heat ratings, on-time prep 15-min escalations, 30-min auto-cancels, quality complaints Owner audit flag, loss of chef incentive bonuses
👤 Customer Smooth delivery acceptance, online prepayments Doorstep COD refusal, repeated order cancellations COD feature permanently revoked (Prepaid only)
🏪 Shop Owner High menu availability, timely rider cash settlement Frequent unannounced offline status, order rejections Store search ranking penalty in customer view
6. Customer Multi-Staff Feedback Engine
Dual Chef & Rider Recognition Performance Linked

Upon delivery completion, customer rates and tags both staff members independently:

  • 👨‍🍳 Kitchen Chef Rating (1–5 ⭐): Piping Hot, Divine Vedic Taste, Spill-Proof Sealed, Pure Desi Ghee ➔ Directly updates Chef's CIBIL Points.
  • 🛵 Delivery Sarathi Rating (1–5 ⭐): Super Fast, Humble & Polite, Safe & Contactless, Easy Navigation ➔ Directly updates Rider's CIBIL Points.
