Foody Vrinda: Operational & Technical Architecture

Comprehensive specifications for Order State Machines, ETA-Matched Dispatch, Physical Handover Controls, Cash Reconciliation, and Immutable Audit Trails.

🛒
1. Customer Lifecycle & Dynamic Address Management
Verified authentication, multi-address selection & geofenced doorstep OTP
  • Pre-Authentication: All orders require Google / Phone verification session tokens. Zero guest checkout fraud.
  • Saved Address Portfolio: Customers manage multiple addresses (Home 🏠, Work 🏢, Ashram/Hostel 📍) with precise Landmark, Unit/Floor, and Delivery Instructions selectable per order.
  • Single-Kitchen Integrity: Enforced 1 order = 1 kitchen = 1 rider constraint to prevent cross-town multi-stop food cooling.
  • Geofenced Delivery Verification: Rider must be within 150m of the delivery location + submit customer's 4-digit OTP to complete trip. Eliminates remote false-delivery abuse.
PLACED ➔ PREPARING ➔ PACKED ➔ OUT_FOR_DELIVERY ➔ DELIVERED
🍳
2. Kitchen Operations & Dynamic ETA Dispatch Window
Intelligent prep timing matching, physical checklist & FSSAI thermal safety
  • Dynamic Prep & Rider ETA Matching: System predicts kitchen prep time (predicted_ready_at) and calculates rider travel time (rider_arrival_eta). Rider is dispatched at the optimal time window so food is packed right as rider arrives.
  • Physical Package Multi-Check: Prevents packet mix-ups via 4-Digit Pickup OTP + Order Short Code + Customer Name + Item checklist validation before handover.
  • Food Safety & Thermal Standards (FSSAI Aligned): Tracks timestamps across cooked_at, packed_at, picked_up_at, and delivered_at ensuring hot holding (>65°C) and fresh transit.
  • Awaiting Pickup Queue: Packed orders remain in kitchen view showing Rider Pickup OTP: XXXX until the Sarathi physically enters the code.
⚡ 15-Min Urgency Alarm: If kitchen neglects an order for 15 minutes, Owner receives an emergency audio alarm. At 30 minutes, COD orders auto-cancel.
🛵
3. Delivery Sarathi Workflow & Daily Security Tokens
Atomic first-arrival claiming, 24h rotating badges & offline resilience
  • Atomic First-Arrival Claiming: Multiple nearby riders receive early alerts. First rider at the counter enters the 4-digit Pickup OTP, instantly executing an atomic lock on rider_id, rider_name, and picked_up_at.
  • Daily Rotating Sarathi Token: 24-hour rotating badge (SR-XXXX) verifying partner identity for staff and customers.
  • Expanded Rider States: OFFLINE, AVAILABLE, ASSIGNED, NAVIGATING_TO_KITCHEN, WAITING_AT_KITCHEN, OUT_FOR_DELIVERY, DELIVERED.
  • Offline Resilient Queue: Local state caching with timestamped verification tokens that synchronize automatically when network recovers.
💰
4. Cash on Delivery Reconciliation & Dispute Management
Full cash variance tracking and dual-confirmation deposit protocol
  • Full Ledger Accounting: Tracks cash_collected, cash_returned, net_cash, cash_expected, cash_settled, and cash_variance.
  • Dual-Confirmation Evening Handover:
    • Rider deposits cash ➔ Cashier enters amount ➔ System verifies match.
    • If discrepancy occurs, flagged as DISPUTED with settlement ID, cashier timestamp, and optional receipt upload for fair manager review.

6. Privacy, Security & Immutable Audit Reporting
Ephemeral customer tracking, immutable event logging & 1-tap CSV export
  • Ephemeral GPS Tracking: Customers only receive live GPS updates while the rider is actively delivering. Long-term customer location history is strictly restricted.
  • Immutable Append-Only Audit Trail: Every milestone (ORDER_CREATED, COOKING_STARTED, PACKED, PICKUP_VERIFIED, OUT_FOR_DELIVERY, DELIVERY_VERIFIED) is recorded with immutable server timestamps.
  • One-Click CSV / Excel Export: Dashboard provides exportDeliveryAuditReportCSV generating a complete audit trail of order IDs, chef packing times, Sarathi pickup codes, delivery durations, and COD settlement status.
