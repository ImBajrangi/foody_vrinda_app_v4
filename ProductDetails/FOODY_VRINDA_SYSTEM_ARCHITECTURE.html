Foody Vrinda: Two-Stage Verification & Chain-of-Custody

Complete guide to Kitchen Handover, Rider Selection, Daily Security Tokens & Audit Reports.

🛒
1. The Customer (Order & Live Tracking)
Pre-authenticated, instant checkout & doorstep delivery verification
  • Must Login First: Zero fake guest orders. 1-tap Google/Phone authentication.
  • Saved Once, Pre-filled Forever: Phone number and address persist seamlessly across orders.
  • Live Map Tracking: Live GPS courier location, cooking status, and ETA.
  • Doorstep Delivery OTP: Customer has a secret 4-digit code (XXXX) on screen. Rider marks delivery complete only when customer confirms receipt.
🍳
2. The Kitchen (Cooking, Hot Food & Handover)
Zero-error pickup queue & dispatch protection
  • Parallel Dispatch: The moment chef clicks "Start Cooking", active riders are alerted. Rider arrives right when food is packed hot.
  • Awaiting Sarathi Pickup Queue: When food is packed, order stays in the kitchen queue displaying Rider Pickup OTP: XXXX until the Sarathi physically enters the code.
  • Zero Parcel Mix-ups: Rider cannot dispatch without the exact 4-digit Pickup OTP shown on the kitchen screen.
  • Auto-Clear: The instant Rider verifies the OTP, Supabase Realtime removes the order from Kitchen Screen into active transit.
⚡ 15-Min Urgency Alarm: If kitchen neglects an order for 15 minutes, Owner receives an emergency audio alarm. At 30 minutes, COD orders auto-cancel.
🛵
3. Delivery Sarathi (Rider Selection & Daily Token)
First-arrival physical OTP locking & daily rotating security badge
  • Daily Rotating Sarathi Token: Each rider receives a 24-hour rotating badge (SR-XXXX) verifying their identity for staff and customers.
  • Stage 1 (Kitchen Pickup Lock): First Sarathi to arrive enters Kitchen screen's 4-digit Pickup OTP in their interactive modal. Order instantly locks to that Sarathi's name, phone, and wallet.
  • Stage 2 (Doorstep Customer Delivery): Sarathi reaches doorstep, takes the 4-digit Delivery OTP from the customer, collects COD cash (if applicable), and completes the order.
  • Audit Export: Sarathi & Admin can export the full delivery audit report to CSV with a single tap.
💰
4. Cash on Delivery (Evening Settlement)
Daily digital cash ledger without reconciliation discrepancies
  • Digital Wallet: Tracks daily COD cash collection in real-time.
  • Evening Handover to Shopkeeper:
    • Paid in Full (100%): Account cleared to ₹0. Rider gets +15 Trust Points.
    • Paid Partial (Shortage): Remaining balance stays as debt. 24h grace period applies, with -20 Trust Points deducted.
    • Paid Extra: Stored in rider's advance wallet.
📊

Incentivizes excellence, blocks fraudulent behavior

⭐
6. Separate Ratings for Chef & Rider
Fair credit for kitchen taste vs delivery behavior
  • Chef Rating (1–5 ⭐): Taste, freshness, packaging & hot food quality.
  • Rider Rating (1–5 ⭐): Delivery speed, polite communication & punctuality.
  • Real Impact: 5-star ratings directly award bonus Trust points to that individual staff member.
