================================================================================
🌟 FOODY VRINDA - PRODUCTION-GRADE SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE & OPERATIONAL GUIDE (v5.0)
================================================================================

1. 🛒 CUSTOMER (Order Lifecycle, Multi-Address & Secure Verification)
• Authentication: 1-Tap Google / Phone login with verified session tokens. Zero unverified guest orders.
• Dynamic Address Management: Multiple saved addresses (Home 🏠, Work 🏢, Ashram/Hostel 📍) with precise Landmark, Unit/Floor, and Delivery Instructions. Address can be switched per order.
• Single-Kitchen Policy: Enforced 1 order = 1 kitchen = 1 rider to prevent multi-hop cross-city food spoilage.
• Real-Time Order Milestones:
  [CREATED] ➔ [PREPARING] ➔ [PACKED] ➔ [OUT_FOR_DELIVERY] ➔ [DELIVERED]
• Doorstep OTP & Geofence Protection:
  - Customer receives a 4-Digit Delivery OTP on the tracking screen.
  - Delivery completion requires Rider to be within the delivery geofence (<150m) + valid OTP entry to eliminate premature delivery abuse.


2. 🍳 KITCHEN & FOOD SAFETY (Dynamic Prep Timing, Packaging & Physical QC)
• Scoped Kitchen Screen: Each kitchen strictly accesses orders for their specific branch.
• Intelligent Dynamic Dispatch Window:
  - Replaces naive parallel dispatch.
  - System dynamically matches Kitchen Prep ETA (`predicted_ready_at`) with Rider Travel ETA (`rider_arrival_eta`).
  - Rider is dispatched only at the optimal window (`dispatch_at`) so the rider arrives exactly when the food is freshly packed.
• Physical Package Multi-Factor Verification:
  - Kitchen card displays 4-Digit Pickup OTP, Order Short Code, Customer Name, and Item Checklist.
  - Staff verifies dish checklist and package label before handover.
• Food Safety & Temperature Retention (FSSAI Aligned):
  - Hot food holding standards (>65°C) and thermal transport compliance.
  - Timestamps recorded: `cooked_at`, `packed_at`, `picked_up_at`, `delivered_at`.
• Urgency Safety Timers: 15-minute kitchen inactivity alarm to Owner; automatic escalation if kitchen response stalls.


3. 🛵 DELIVERY SARATHI (Rider Flow, Daily Security Badge & Atomic Claiming)
• Comprehensive Rider Availability States:
  [OFFLINE] ➔ [AVAILABLE] ➔ [ASSIGNED] ➔ [NAVIGATING_TO_KITCHEN] ➔ [WAITING_AT_KITCHEN] ➔ [OUT_FOR_DELIVERY] ➔ [DELIVERED]
• Atomic First-Arrival Claiming:
  - Multiple nearby riders receive early route alerts.
  - First rider at the counter verifies the Kitchen Pickup OTP; database executes an atomic lock binding `rider_id`, `rider_name`, `rider_phone`, and `picked_up_at`. Zero double-assignment race conditions.
• Daily Rotating Sarathi Token:
  - Daily display identifier (`SR-XXXX`, e.g. `SR-8921`) rotating every 24h for quick visual verification by staff and customers.
• Offline Resilience Strategy:
  - Local state caching, optimistic UI updates, and signed timestamp tokens that reconcile automatically when network connectivity resumes.


4. 💰 COD CASH RECONCILIATION & DISPUTE MECHANISM
• Real-World Cash Accounting Ledger:
  - Tracks `cash_collected`, `cash_returned`, `net_cash`, `cash_expected`, `cash_settled`, `cash_variance`.
• Evening Settlement Dual-Confirmation Workflow:
  - Rider initiates deposit ➔ Cashier enters received amount ➔ Dual-confirmation match.
  - If variance occurs: System flags as `DISPUTED` with settlement ID, cashier ID, timestamp, and optional receipt upload for fair manager review (no instant unfair penalty).


5. 📊 FOODY OPERATIONAL TRUST SCORE (300 – 900 Points)
*Note: This is an internal platform reliability score and is NOT a credit bureau score.*
• Context-Aware Delay Attribution:
  - Scores are adjusted ONLY when delay responsibility is verified (`KITCHEN_DELAY`, `RIDER_DELAY`, `TRAFFIC_DELAY`, `WEATHER_DELAY`, `CUSTOMER_DELAY`).
  - Heavy traffic, rain, or kitchen backlogs do not penalize innocent delivery sarathis.
• Graduated Customer COD Policy:
  - 1st failed/unreachable delivery: Warning & customer support callback.
  - 2nd failure: Temporary COD hold with review.
  - Chronic fraud/refusal: Transition to Prepaid-only mode (No harsh 1-strike bans).


6. 🔒 PRIVACY, SECURITY & DATA RETENTION
• Ephemeral Live Tracking: Customers see live rider position only while an order is actively in transit.
• Data Minimization: Customer location history is kept confidential and separated from long-term public query logs.
• OTP Security: Rate-limited verification (max 5 attempts), short-lived expiry, and one-time invalidation.


7. 🛡️ IMMUTABLE CHAIN-OF-CUSTODY & AUDIT REPORTING
• Append-Only Lifecycle Events:
  - Order milestones (`ORDER_CREATED`, `COOKING_STARTED`, `PACKED`, `PICKUP_VERIFIED`, `OUT_FOR_DELIVERY`, `DELIVERY_VERIFIED`) recorded with immutable server timestamps.
• 1-Click CSV/Excel Audit Export:
  - Available on dashboard for Owners and Admins to audit timings, route durations, OTP logs, and cash settlement status.
