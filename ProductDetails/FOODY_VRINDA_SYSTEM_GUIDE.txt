🌟 FOODY VRINDA - PRODUCTION-GRADE SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE & RISK MITIGATION BLUEPRINT (v5.1)
================================================================================

1. 🛒 CUSTOMER (Order Lifecycle, Authentication & Privacy Protections)
• Strict Pre-Authentication: Orders require verified user session tokens (user_id NOT NULL). Zero anonymous/guest orders to eliminate fake order spam.
• Dynamic Address Management: Multiple saved addresses (Home 🏠, Work 🏢, Ashram/Hostel 📍) with precise Landmark, Unit/Floor, and Delivery Instructions. Selectable per checkout.
• Single-Kitchen Policy: Enforced 1 order = 1 kitchen = 1 rider to prevent multi-hop cross-city food spoilage.
• Real-Time 16-State Lifecycle:
  [NEW] ➔ [PAYMENT_PENDING] ➔ [CONFIRMED] ➔ [ACCEPTED] ➔ [COOKING] ➔ [READY_FOR_PICKUP] ➔ [RIDER_ASSIGNED] ➔ [RIDER_ARRIVING] ➔ [PICKED_UP] ➔ [OUT_FOR_DELIVERY] ➔ [DELIVERED]
  (Failure paths: [DELIVERY_ATTEMPTED_FAILED], [DISPUTED], [CANCELLED], [REFUND_PENDING], [REFUNDED])
• Rider Contact Privacy: Rider phone numbers are proxy/masked via in-app calling channel.
• Doorstep OTP & Tri-Tier Geofence Protection:
  - Customer receives a 4-Digit Delivery OTP on the tracking screen.
  - Geofence Tiers:
    • < 100m: Normal doorstep verification.
    • 100m – 250m: Proximity alert & customer location confirmation prompt.
    • > 250m: Delivery lock. Requires controlled support override with an audit event.


2. 🍳 KITCHEN & FOOD SAFETY (Dynamic Prep Timing, Packaging & Physical QC)
• Scoped Kitchen Screen: Each kitchen strictly accesses orders for their specific branch (shop_id).
• Dynamic Prep & Continuous ETA Recalculation:
  - Kitchen preparation time (predicted_ready_at) is continuously matched against rider travel ETA (predicted_rider_arrival_at) with an eta_confidence_score.
  - Rider is dispatched at the optimal dispatch window (dispatch_at) so the rider arrives exactly when the dish is freshly packed.
  - Structured Kitchen Delays: Delays require explicit reason codes (e.g., INGREDIENT_DELAY, RUSH_HOUR_SURGE, POWER_FLUCTUATION) rather than arbitrary unmonitored clicks.
• Quality-Control Transit Threshold:
  - Transit time alerts (35-45 mins context-aware) flag thermal quality degradation without oversimplifying biological food safety.
• Machine-Verifiable Physical Handover:
  - Handover requires a 4-factor check: Package QR/Barcode + Order Short Code + 4-Digit Pickup OTP + Staff item checklist.
• Urgency Safety Timers: 15-minute kitchen inactivity alarm to Owner; automatic escalation if kitchen response stalls.


3. 🛵 DELIVERY SARATHI (Rider Flow, Daily Security Badge & Atomic Claiming)
• Comprehensive Rider Availability States:
  [OFFLINE] ➔ [AVAILABLE] ➔ [ASSIGNED] ➔ [NAVIGATING_TO_KITCHEN] ➔ [WAITING_AT_KITCHEN] ➔ [OUT_FOR_DELIVERY] ➔ [DELIVERED]
• Atomic First-Arrival Claiming:
  - Multiple nearby riders receive early route alerts.
  - First rider at the counter verifies the Kitchen Pickup OTP; database executes an atomic lock binding rider_id, rider_name, rider_phone, and picked_up_at. Zero double-assignment race conditions.
• Daily Rotating Sarathi Token:
  - Daily display identifier (SR-XXXX, e.g. SR-8921) rotating every 24h for quick visual verification by staff and customers.
• Offline Resilience Strategy & Action Partitioning:
  - Safe to queue locally: ARRIVED_AT_KITCHEN, NAVIGATION_STARTED, CUSTOMER_CONTACT_ATTEMPTED.
  - Require online server verification: PICKUP_VERIFIED, DELIVERY_OTP_VERIFIED, CASH_SETTLED, ORDER_DELIVERED.
• Doorstep Proof & Photo Privacy:
  - For failed delivery attempts, rider captures in-app camera proof (stored in private cloud bucket, never personal phone gallery, auto-purged after 7 days).


4. 💰 COD MULTI-ACTOR CASH RECONCILIATION LEDGER
• Real-World Cash Accounting:
  - Captures: cash_received_from_customer, change_returned_to_customer, net_collected, expected_amount, variance.
• Multi-Actor Dual Confirmation Handover:
  - Rider declares amount (rider_declared_amount, declared_by).
  - Cashier enters received amount (cashier_received_amount, received_by).
  - Manager approves (approved_by).
  - Mismatch triggers DISPUTED settlement status with audit event, cashier ID, timestamp, and receipt image.


5. 📊 FOODY OPERATIONAL TRUST SCORE (300 – 900 Points)
*Note: This is an internal platform reliability score and is NOT a credit bureau score.*
• Context-Aware Delay Attribution:
  - Scores are adjusted ONLY when delay responsibility is verified (KITCHEN_DELAY, RIDER_DELAY, TRAFFIC_DELAY, WEATHER_DELAY, CUSTOMER_DELAY).
  - Heavy traffic, rain, or kitchen backlogs do not penalize innocent delivery sarathis.
• Graduated Customer COD Policy:
  - 1st failed/unreachable delivery: Warning & customer support callback.
  - 2nd failure: Temporary COD hold with review.
  - Chronic fraud/refusal: Transition to Prepaid-only mode (No harsh 1-strike bans).


6. 🔒 DATA INTEGRITY, IMMUTABLE LEDGER & RLS ACCESS CONTROL
• Cryptographic Hash Chaining on Audit Events (foody_order_events):
  - Schema contains previous_event_hash and event_hash.
  - Foreign key uses ON DELETE RESTRICT (order cancellation != database deletion; audit logs never disappear).
  - PostgreSQL trigger trg_prevent_audit_tamper blocks all UPDATE and DELETE operations.
• Zero-Plaintext Hashed OTPs:
  - pickup_otp_hash and delivery_otp_hash with pickup_otp_expires_at, delivery_otp_expires_at, and attempt rate-limiting counters.
• Row-Level Security (RLS) Strict Policies:
  - Customer: Own orders and active live transit only.
  - Kitchen/Chef: Assigned branch orders only.
  - Rider: Available orders in area & assigned delivery tasks.
  - Owner: Complete shop-scoped records & financial ledgers.
  - Admin: Full system telemetry and audit reports.
• 1-Click CSV/Excel Audit Export:
  - Instant generation of order lifecycle audit logs, timestamps, pickup codes, durations, and settlement records.
