🌟 FOODY VRINDA - COMPLETE SYSTEM AT A GLANCE (v4.2)
=======================================================

1. 🛒 CUSTOMER (Order & Live Tracking)
• No fake orders: Must login (Google / Phone) before checkout.
• One-time save: Phone number and address save once, pre-fill forever.
• Live tracking: Real-time map tracking with live food cooking & courier route.
• 4-Digit Doorstep Delivery OTP: Customer shares secret OTP shown in their app with Rider at doorstep to confirm delivery.


2. 🍳 KITCHEN & STAFF (Cooking, Packaging & Handover)
• Scoped Screen: Each kitchen only sees dishes for their branch.
• HOT FOOD GUARANTEE (Parallel Dispatch): The moment chef clicks "Start Cooking", nearby riders are alerted immediately. While food cooks (10-15m), rider reaches the shop. Food is picked up burning hot!
• Packing & Ready State: When packed, order stays in "Awaiting Sarathi Pickup" state with the 4-Digit Rider Pickup OTP.
• Handover Verification: When Rider arrives, staff provides the 4-digit Pickup OTP. Rider enters it to lock order dispatch (0% wrong parcel handover).
• 15m/30m Safety: If kitchen ignores an order for 15 mins, Owner gets alarm. At 30 mins, COD orders auto-cancel.


3. 🛵 DELIVERY SARATHI (Rider Flow & Daily Security Badge)
• Nearest radar: Closest active rider gets the order ping.
• Daily Rotating Sarathi Token: Har Sarathi ka 24h rotating digital badge hota hai (e.g. SR-8921) jo unki verified identity confirm karta hai.
• 2-Step OTP Security & Instant Locking:
  - Step 1 (Pickup Lock): Rider counter par aakar Kitchen screen ka 4-Digit Pickup OTP enter karta hai. OTP match hote hi order usi Rider ke naam lock ho jata hai.
  - Step 2 (Delivery Handover): Customer ke doorstep par Customer ka 4-Digit Delivery OTP enter karke delivery finish hoti hai.
• 1-Click Audit Report Download: Rider / Admin CSV audit report download kar sakte hain jisme timing, routes, aur OTP records hote hain.


4. 💰 COD CASH SETTLEMENT (Evening Handover)
• Rider collects cash all day -> System tracks exact total in digital wallet.
• Full Settlement (100%): Account cleared to Rs.0, Rider gets +15 Trust Points.
• Partial Settlement (Shortage): Balance stays as debt, 24h grace period, late penalty applies, and -20 Trust points deducted.
• Excess: Saved in rider's advance wallet.


5. 📊 CIBIL TRUST SCORE (300 to 900 Points)
• Rider: Good deposits & fast delivery boost score. Short cash cuts score & blocks COD orders.
• Chef: Fast cooking & 5-star ratings boost score. 15-min delays cut score.
• Customer: Refusing COD orders at doorstep permanently BANS Cash on Delivery (Prepaid only).


6. ⭐ SEPARATE REVIEWS FOR CHEF & RIDER
Customer gives separate 1-5 star ratings:
• Chef Rating: Taste, Hot Food & Packaging.
• Rider Rating: Speed & Polite Behavior.
5-star ratings give bonus Trust Points directly to that staff!


7. 🛡️ FULL CHAIN-OF-CUSTODY AUDIT TRAIL
Har order ka complete digital record available rehta hai:
• Chef Name & Exact Packing Timestamp (packed_at)
• Rider Name, Phone, Daily Token (SR-XXXX) & Pickup Timestamp (picked_up_at)
• Realtime GPS Broadcast Transit History
• Customer Delivery Timestamp (delivered_at) & Cash Status
• 1-Click Export to CSV / Excel for Admin and Owner.
